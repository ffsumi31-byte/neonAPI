Set up your connector

  1. Install Claude Code from Anthropic.
  2. Extract the connector and open a terminal in your project directory.
  3. Run the launcher for your operating system.
    PowerShell& "C:\path\to\Start-ApiDuck.ps1"
    Bashbash /path/to/start-apiduck.sh
  4. Enter your sk-gw-… customer key at the hidden prompt. Hosted requests require administrator enablement.

Help

Customer keys, token balances, and hosted access.

Which key do I use?

Use only the sk-gw-… customer key you received from your Apiduh administrator. Provider keys stay on the server.

Do my tokens expire?

Your lifetime token grants do not expire or reset monthly. Additional grants increase your allowance.

What is an open reservation?

Reserved tokens are held for a request in progress or under review. The ledger lists them separately from settled usage.

Why are hosted messages unavailable?

The administrator must validate the live database, provider connection, and quota enforcement before enabling requests.
